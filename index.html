Questions

How do I log in?

Parents and tutors create their own account first (Sign up), then log in. Tutors already listed by TutorMatch have a Tutor ID (like TM-T012); they activate it once with a code from the admin and choose their own password.

Is my phone number safe?

A tutor's number stays hidden until the tutor accepts your request. Parents' numbers are never collected.

Which classes, boards and streams are covered?

Classes 1-12 with CBSE, ICSE and MP Board, English or Hindi medium, and Science, Commerce and Arts streams for Classes 11-12.

Are the demo tutors real people?

No. They are fictional and marked DEMO. Real tutors sign up, are verified by an admin, and then show a Verified badge.
